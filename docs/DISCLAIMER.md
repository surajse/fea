# Disclaimer

FEA (Forward Engineer Anything) is a **neutral engineering tool**. It helps
you describe observed software behavior, reimplement that behavior in a
clean-room workflow, and measure behavioral parity between an original and a
rebuild.

**You — the operator — are responsible for authorization.** Only point FEA at
software you own, that is open-source, that you are contracted or licensed to
analyze, or that you otherwise have a lawful right to inspect (e.g. a CTF lab
you run). FEA's authorization checkpoint is a speed bump, not legal advice.

**Clean-room is a discipline, not a verdict.** The firewall separates analysis
knowledge from synthesis inputs by construction, following the classic
clean-room design. Whether a particular reimplementation is lawful depends on
your jurisdiction, the target's license, and facts FEA cannot see. When in
doubt, ask a lawyer — not a tool.

**Parity is behavioral, not proof.** A score of 100 means the rebuild matched
the original on the tested vectors after documented normalization. It is not a
formal proof of equivalence and must never be presented as one.

By using FEA you agree that its authors and contributors are not liable for
how you use it.
